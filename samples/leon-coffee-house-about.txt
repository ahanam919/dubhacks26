SOURCE 2: Intentionalist business listing
URL: https://intentionalist.com/?p=88373
Lupe Chavez and his family opened Leon Coffee House next to the Neptune Theater in November 2021. The menu has classic espresso drinks alongside Mexican-inspired drinks. The shop also serves sandwiches and sweet and savory waffles.

SOURCE 3: KING 5 feature on the shop
URL: https://king5.com/article/entertainment/television/programs/evening/latte-art-delicious-drinks-leon-coffee-house-seattle-university-district/281-5b6ad329-3218-4733-bf38-b14cef3aaa3d
Leon Coffee House is a two-story shop next to the Neptune Theatre in the University District. Its drinks are topped with hand-poured latte art, such as a bear, cat or bunny. Baristas train for roughly three months to learn the latte art. A barista says the horchata latte is famous and that people travel from other states, such as Oregon, to get it. The menu reflects the owner's and staff's Mexican culture.

SOURCE 4: Seattle Refined review
URL: https://seattlerefined.com/eat-drink/leon-coffee-house-latte-art-vibe-cute-seattle-u-district
The reviewer tried two signature drinks, the Horchata Latte and the Velvet Latte. The food menu includes granola, sandwiches, pork tamales and waffles with whipped cream. The two-story space has Frida Kahlo paintings, neon signs with quotes, and a chandelier with a floral print.
